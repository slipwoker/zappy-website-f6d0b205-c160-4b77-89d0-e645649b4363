    Frequently Asked Questions

    How can I listen to your podcasts?

    How do I order the educational game?

    You can order our game by contacting us through the form above or calling us directly. We'll provide information about pricing, shipping, and bulk order discounts for schools and organizations.

    What age groups are your products suitable for?

    Our podcasts and game are designed primarily for children ages 6-12, though content varies by series. Lectures can be tailored to specific age groups from elementary through middle school, and we offer family-friendly presentations for all ages.

    How do I book a lecture for my school?

    To book a lecture, contact us with your preferred date, audience size, and topic interests. We'll work with you to customize a presentation that meets your needs and schedule. We recommend booking at least 4-6 weeks in advance for best availability.

    Do you offer bulk discounts for schools?

    Yes! We offer special pricing for schools and organizations ordering multiple games or booking multiple lecture sessions. Contact us for a customized quote based on your specific needs.

    What is your response time?

    We typically respond to all inquiries within 1-2 business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly during business hours for immediate assistance.
