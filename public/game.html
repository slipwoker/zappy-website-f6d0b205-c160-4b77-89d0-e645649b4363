    How to Play

    1

    Setup the Board

    Unfold the colorful game board, shuffle the question cards, and each player chooses their game piece. Place all pieces at the starting position.

    2

    Roll and Move

    Players take turns rolling the dice and moving their pieces around the board. Land on different colored spaces for various challenges.

    3

    Answer Questions

    Draw a card matching your space color and answer the educational question or complete the challenge to earn points.

    4

    Win the Game

    The first player to reach the finish line with the required number of points wins! Or play for highest score after all players finish.
